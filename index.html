AFUED Continuous Assessment Harmonized Guidelines

Official policy governing CBT assessments and semester examinations

Senate Approved

1. Mandatory CA Participation

Under Senate regulations, no candidate shall be eligible to sit for the final 70% degree examination without completing the 30% Continuous Assessment components.

2. Anti-Malpractice Proctoring

All CBT tests are proctored via timed sessions with randomized question sequences and automated submission upon expiration.

3. Score Computation & Weighting

Continuous Assessment consists of two structured tests (15 Marks each) or a test plus practical lab report. Total CA = 30 Marks.

4. Official Result Verification

Each printable result slip bears an encrypted verification identifier authenticating the student's standing with the ICT Registry.
